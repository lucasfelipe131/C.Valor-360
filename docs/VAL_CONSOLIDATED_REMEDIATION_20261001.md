# Consolidated correction after maximum capture

The diagnostic cohort at `c97018dbc1a2c3f6cdd476d56b3d44813240ac8a` closed with 299 executed cases, one legitimate blocked case, and no pending independent cases. It does not certify this change. ## Proven causes and changes

- A shared Boolean source gate mapped economic/current-data/credit requirements to a product-label refusal. Source requirements now retain their regulatory, current-data or financial kind through delivery. Economic methodology does not request a live quotation; mixed requests still retain the live-source gate. The grain adapter is not used as a currency source.
- Tool keywords inside a question about method or limitations could execute a calculator, soil-analysis or image-analysis tool. The router now examines the question clause as well as its introductory situation. Explicit records, supplied quantities, attachments and live requests retain their existing boundaries.
- Explicit arithmetic that did not fit the original adapters fell through to the calculator catalog. Bounded operations now cover revenue, margin, net return, percentage change, price/mass conversion, rectangular area, quantity per hectare and incremental/break-even scenarios. Inputs, formulas, output units and hypothetical status are retained. Missing yield produces a material question rather than a computed value. Ambiguous operands, negative operands outside the supported grammar, prescriptions and current facts are not inferred.
- The domain classifier treated `sc/ha`, `saca` and `sacas` differently. It now recognizes these equivalent grain units. No grounding validator, threshold or evidence contract was disabled or relaxed.
- Intentional cancellation left a user turn without a terminal notice. Cancellation now settles only the owning thread and turn, once, without creating a grounded assistant reply. Existing abort, generation and stale-response guards remain.
- A general behavioral explanation could display a personal-profile card solely because its topic was PROFILE. Explicitly general answers retain their normal answer presentation.

## Validation and scope

Generic regressions exercise source separation, mixed-source negative controls, complete arithmetic delivery through grounding, missing inputs, cancellation ownership/idempotency and profile rendering. The existing unsupported-revenue test now checks the supported revenue formula and amount while continuing to reject conversion of that revenue into cost.

Auth, portfolio scope, `getClientContext`, client authorization and fixture ownership were not changed. The only context-selector change normalizes grain unit vocabulary. Preserved G1/G2/G3/G4 automated and PR011 remain preserved; this change does not claim a new human or physical-UAT approval.

Remaining model-output, retrieval/grounding, contextual and private-fact failures are retained in the diagnostic report. Their outcomes require fresh capture; no rejection was relabeled as useful and no private fact was manufactured. The blocked late-response case remains blocked when its exact arrival precondition cannot occur through normal cancellation behavior.
